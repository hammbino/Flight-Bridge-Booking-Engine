# Premium Adventure Experiences

**Status: Idea.** Nothing is decided. Backlog: BE-FB-008. Open: Q-BE-013 (how packages get booked), Q-BE-016 (demand).

## Concept

Curated, experience-based trips to exotic places, not typical beach or Vegas trips. Partners build "manicured" experiences, and Flight Bridge advertises and sells them on its site so clients book through Flight Bridge (PA 00:04:31, 00:08:47).

**About the source:** PA is a monologue by an unnamed "Speaker 1", probably Mauricio (unconfirmed). It ends with "Am I blowing smoke?" and no answer is recorded (PA 00:10:42).

## Why

- Higher price point. People want experiences they cannot get day to day (PA 00:05:02, ASM-BE-011).
- Proof point: an Antarctica trip nobody thought would sell booked a few people (PA 00:08:47).
- High-end clients respond to experience messaging and are not focused on cost (AV 00:06:51).

## Content-first group model

A team member does the experience first and creates content. The next departure runs as a group (PA 00:09:45). Same idea as PARENT NL-FB-011.
