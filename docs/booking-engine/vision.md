# Booking Engine Vision

## In one paragraph

The Flight Bridge Booking Engine lets people book flights on Traverse's own site, over Traverse's direct Sabre access and under its ARC number, with a real agent following up on every booking. It starts as the place newsletter deals land. It grows into self-booking for consumers and corporate travelers. Later it becomes the booking tool other agencies pay to use: "the Picasso, but better".

## Why it matters

- **It is where the money is.** Jeff: bookings are where the real money is made, not subscriptions (M0924 00:05:33). Mauricio called the online booking tool the "lowest hanging fruit" for revenue (SV 00:26:00).
- **It closes the gap competitors leave.** Flights From Home and Going send users to Google Flights or the airline. Neither books (M0924 00:19:08, M0610 00:38:27). Flight Bridge keeps the customer on its own page and adds a human ("White Glove Service", M0924 00:23:19).
- **It is now possible.** Traverse has direct Sabre access (SV 00:26:00).
- **It feeds the B2B business.** Most small agencies lack an ARC and book through hosts. Flight Bridge can be an easier, integrated host (M0924 00:46:04 to 00:47:15).

## Platform context

Flight Bridge is a platform company. It licenses its newsletter, CRM and booking engine stack to agencies under a white-label model. Traverse Tours is the first licensee (PARENT docs/newsletter/vision.md). Customer-facing pages read "Traverse Tours powered by Flight Bridge" (M0610 01:09:19).

## Long-term vision

A clean UI over Sabre: type a city, pick dates, see results, click to book, ticket issued. This is modeled on the shell Mauricio used at Continental Airlines (SV 00:10:23). Eventually agents do all Sabre work in the Flight Bridge UI (BE-FB-007). Flight Bridge will never build its own GDS (DEC-BE-001).
