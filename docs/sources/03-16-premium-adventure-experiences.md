# Premium Adventure Experiences

**Date:** 03-16  **Participants:** Speaker 1 only (monologue). The transcript does not name the speaker. Earlier parent-repo docs attribute it to Mauricio Melendez. The transcript supports that: the speaker says "Jeff, maybe you would know" and "you guys", so the speaker is not Jeff, and Jeff is among the listeners. Still unconfirmed by name. The speaker refers to "my team members", "partners" and "our website", which fits an agency owner.
**Aliases (identical files in parent repo Transcripts/):** "03-16 From Ideation to Productized Premium Adventure Experiences_ A Decision Framework and Go-To-Market Plan-transcript.txt", "Premium Adventure Experiences" (alias differs only by extra blank lines)
**Booking relevance:** Medium. It is the origin of the idea to sell curated premium adventure experiences on the website, but it is a one-person brainstorm with no decisions.

## Summary for agents
The speaker wants to put together experience-based adventure trips to exotic places, not standard beach or Vegas trips. Reasons given: a higher price point, and people are drawn to experiences they cannot get every day. The idea is that partners build "manicured" experiences, and Flight Bridge advertises and sells them on its website so clients book through Flight Bridge. A team member would go first to make content, and later trips would run with a group. Nothing is decided. The speaker is asking for feedback on whether anyone would pay for this. The recording is a monologue, so no answers are captured.
