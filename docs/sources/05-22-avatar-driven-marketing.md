# Avatar-Driven Marketing

**Date:** 05-22  **Participants:** Mauricio Melendez (the only labeled speaker), Jeffrey Hammond (his replies are merged into Mauricio's turns)
**Aliases (identical files in parent repo Transcripts/):** "05-22 Avatar-Driven Marketing Flight-Bridge_ Shifting from Unstable Revenue to a Predictable Sales Model by Targeting the Economic Buyer-transcript.txt", "Avatar-Driven Marketing Flight-Bridge" (alias differs only by extra blank lines)
**Booking relevance:** Low. This is marketing coaching, mostly about a third party's business ("Dustin"). The only booking angle is that the person who pays for a trip is often not the traveler.

## Summary for agents
Mauricio advises predictable monthly recurring revenue over large lump sums. This advice is about another person's business, not Flight Bridge bookings. He then explains avatar-driven marketing from his past agency work. For honeymoons, the couple is often young with low income, and parents or grandparents pay. So marketing targets the economic buyer, not the traveler. For high-end clients, messaging centered on experience and gastronomy and never mentioned cost. Nothing here was decided for the booking engine.
